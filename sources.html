    DDerived in this material

    • Unit conversions: °C = (°F − 32)/1.8; barg = psig × 0.06895. E.g. 991 °F → 532.8 °C; 1341 °F → 727.2 °C; 1355 °F → 735.0 °C; 30 psig → 2.07 barg; 33 psig → 2.28 barg.
    • Ratios from book data: riser C/O = 144/20 = 7.2 and H/D = 33/0.8 ≈ 41 (Ex. E8.4); C/O ≈ 12 implied by Ex. E8.1 + E8.2.
    • Correlation check: every Ex. E8.1 value was recomputed from Table 8.6 to confirm the signs, which are lost in the PDF text; this also found the HCO misprint (9.7 printed, 9.2 computed).
    • Three-lump chart: Eqs 8.19–8.21 integrated numerically (RK4, φ = 1) with the book's k-values. Gasoline peaks at about 45.6 wt% near 75 % conversion.
    • Feed preheat lever: reasoned from the reactor heat balance, Eq. 8.17.
