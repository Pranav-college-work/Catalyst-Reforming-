    6.5Operating modes and constraints

    Max gasoline

    • Feed 446 °F (230 °C), C/O 5.4
    • Riser out 991 °F (533 °C); regen 1341 °F (727 °C)
    • 30 / 33 psig; dispersion steam 0.9 wt%

    BTable 8.4

    Max light olefins

    • Severe riser + blended catalyst (Petro-FCC) or DCC/CPP; ZSM-5
    • Propylene 22 vs 4.7 wt%; naphtha 28 vs 53.5 wt% (table in 1.6)

    B§8.16, Table 8.8, p. 209

    More LCO (middle distillate)

    • Lower conversion: LCO 20.7 LV% at 55 % vs 15.8 at 75 %
    • LCO needs hydrotreating for diesel

    DTable 8.6 BFig 8.1

    Constraints

    • Heat balance: coke yield not free. Bp. 211 Regen burning capacity vs high-CCR feed. Bp. 201
    • High coke laydown: more air/O₂, higher regen T, more heat removal. Lp. 20 Regen T ceiling: overheating kills catalyst. Lp. 16
    • Metals: V kills activity; Ni → coke + H₂. Lp. 20

    6.6Conclusion: 8 lines

    1. Heavy gas oil (316–566 °C) → gasoline, LPG olefins, cycle oils on circulating fluidised zeolite. Bpp. 199–200
    2. Riser: 480–550 °C, 1–5 bar, 2–10 s, pneumatic transport, ~plug flow. Bpp. 208, 210
    3. Regenerator burns coke at 620–845 °C; hot catalyst carries heat back. Lp. 17
    4. Catalyst: RE-exchanged zeolite Y in kaolin/binder matrix, ~70 µm; ZSM-5 for octane + olefins. Lpp. 13, 16 Bp. 209
    5. Mechanism: protonation → β-scission → 1°→3° rearrangement → hydride transfer. Lpp. 11–12
    6. Net endothermic; coke yield set by heat balance. Bpp. 208, 211
    7. Gasoline 35–60, LPG 8–20, LCO 12–20, HCO + slurry 10–15, dry gas 3–5, coke 3–5 wt%. BTable 8.2
    8. Optimise: T, C/O, short contact, catalyst, full CO₂ burn, hydrotreated feed. Lpp. 17–20 B§8.7

    6.7Quick check

    Define conversion.
    (feed − cycle stock)/feed × 100, by volume; cycle stock = LCO + HCO. BEq. 8.12
    Why can volume yield exceed 100 %?
    Products are less dense than feed. Lp. 23
    Three ways to raise gasoline?
    Zeolite; short contact + fast separation; optimum (not max) riser T; hydrotreated feed; full CO₂ regeneration. Lpp. 12–20
    More propylene — what change?
    ZSM-5, higher severity, or DCC / Petro-FCC (22 vs 4.7 wt%). B§8.16, Table 8.8
